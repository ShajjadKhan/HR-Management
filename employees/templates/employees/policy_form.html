Individual Contract-Based Leave System (Saudi Labor Law & Manpower Contracting)

In contracting and manpower supply, leave entitlement is not a flat bulk policy. Each worker has an individual contract commitment (e.g., 21 days standard labor, 30 days for engineers/supervisors, or 42 days for 2-year cycles). The form below sets the Company Baseline Default Template applied when registering new workers. You can customize or review each worker's individual contract entitlement in the roster below or directly on their profile.
