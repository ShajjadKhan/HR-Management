Principle of Least Privilege & Anti-Spam Security Protocol

• Isolated Governance: This dashboard is strictly scoped to identity verification, spam prevention, and job requirements moderation. It cannot access tenant company bank accounts, salary disbursements, or enter customer tenant data.
• Tamper-Proof Provenance: Every candidate registration requires a single-use cryptographically bound invite token. Replay attacks and client-side DOM inspection modifications are strictly rejected by the server.
• Anti-Crime Audit: Master Admin and Staff Moderators can revoke suspicious invitation keys with one click and suspend abusive accounts instantly.
